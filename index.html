Profile

About Me

Industrial designer turned AM specialist, bringing 20+ years of product development experience to advanced 3D printing. For the past decade, I've focused on turning AM into real products: helmets, impact-absorbing lattices, and non-planar FDM workflows.

As founder/co-founder of KUPOL, KOLLIDE/K3D, and nonplanar.xyz, I've led the full journey from R&D to commercial products, defining vision, building AM workflows, collaborating with OEMs, and contributing as inventor to multiple international patent families.

Passionate about shaping AM strategy, unlocking new applications, and bridging R&D, product, and business in established or scaling companies.

Co-Founder Montreal, Canada

KOLLIDE is a Montreal-based collective and company focused on developing advanced 3D-printed football helmet technology. K3D is the 3D-printed liner platform developed within KOLLIDE.

  • Led the 3D printing strategy and parametric design workflows for football helmet liners and related impact systems.
  • Developed and refined 3D lattice architectures tuned for specific impact zones, player positions and comfort requirements.
  • Guided the design of the K3D liner technology integrated into commercial football helmets (LIGHT helmets), now achieving #2 NFL 2025 position-specific rankings (QB and OL) and #1 Virginia Tech ratings in Varsity and Youth categories.
  • Contributed to helmets representing 4 out of 11 five-star varsity helmets in Virginia Tech's updated 2025 rating system.
  • Worked closely with simulation teams, material experts and testing partners to iterate rapidly between design, print and impact test.
  • Contributed as inventor to patent families on cushion pads and bladder systems for helmets and other protective equipment.
  • Represented KOLLIDE in media and industry events, explaining the technology behind 3D-printed helmet liners and their benefits for player safety.
2019 – 2022

nonplanar.xyz

Founder (e-commerce spin-off) Web-site

nonplanar.xyz started as an internal R&D project at KUPOL and evolved into a transactional online platform offering tools for makers interested in non-planar FDM printing.

  • Developed and productized dedicated hardware and tools for non-planar printing (elongated nozzles, accessories, example G-code and workflows).
  • Launched and operated an online shop targeting the maker and 3D-printing enthusiast community, providing ready-to-use products for non-planar FDM experiments.
  • Created educational content and documentation to help users adopt non-planar workflows on standard desktop 3D printers.
  • Positioned nonplanar.xyz as a recognizable reference for non-planar printing within the AM and maker communities.
2017 – Present

KUPOL INC.

Founder & President / Industrial Designer Montreal, Canada

KUPOL develops innovative helmets and protective structures using 3D printing as a core technology.

  • Conceived and developed one of the first fully 3D-printed bike helmets, integrating a multi-layer energy management system and complex lattice structures.
  • Led the end-to-end product development: industrial design, CAD, lattice design, integration of straps and retention systems, ventilation strategy and overall product architecture.
  • Collaborated with HP and Sculpteo to validate production workflows using MJF (Multi Jet Fusion) technology and to showcase KUPOL as a case study in advanced 3D-printed helmets.
  • Created and refined 3D-printed structures for other applications (e.g., insoles, body protection concepts).
  • Incubated nonplanar.xyz as an internal KUPOL R&D initiative, which later evolved into an independent transactional online platform for makers focused on non-planar FDM printing.
  • Contributed as inventor to patent families covering 3D-printed helmets and shock-absorbing assemblies.

Awards & Recognition

Achievements

Virginia Tech Helmet Ratings | #1 Varsity

The LIGHT Apache achieved the highest safety score (0.46) of all varsity helmets. After stricter 2025 standards, only 11 of 33 helmets kept five stars. 4 are LIGHT models with K3D.

2025 · Independent Safety Validation

K3D's 3D-printed lattice structures absorb impact through controlled deformation, dissipating energy layer by layer. 40% lighter than traditional foam helmets while reducing significantly more impact energy.

Virginia Tech Helmet Ratings | #1 Youth

The Apache Lightning leads youth helmet safety while weighing under 3 lbs. After stricter 2025 standards, only 6 of 26 youth helmets retained five-star ratings. Apache Lightning ranked #1.

2025 · Independent Safety Validation

Features next-gen EPP pods for top-tier protection, ventilation, and comfort. The lightest five-star youth helmet available, helping young athletes stay agile while maximizing safety.

NFL 2025 Position-Specific Testing | #2 Quarterbacks

The Apache Pro was named the top-performing NEW helmet in 2025 NFL/NFLPA testing for quarterbacks, ranking #2 overall. Now in all 32 NFL team locker rooms.

2025 · NFL/NFLPA Laboratory Testing

Equipped with 20 customizable impact zones and 3D-printed pods. The technology behind it contributed to 2024 recording the fewest concussions in NFL history, a 17% reduction from 2023.

NFL 2025 | #2 Offensive Linemen

The Gladiator Thunder ranked #2 for offensive linemen in 2025 NFL/NFLPA testing. Position-specific helmets address unique impacts linemen face. Nearly 30% of eligible players now wear them.

2025 · NFL/NFLPA Laboratory Testing

Developed using K3D's advanced simulation tools to recreate and optimize for the specific impacts linemen experience. Weighs up to 40% less than competitors, reducing kinetic energy in collisions.

NFL Helmet Challenge | Two Consecutive Grants

KOLLIDE won two consecutive NFL grants: $238,545 (HeadHealthTECH 2020) and $550,000 (Helmet Challenge 2021, largest award), totaling $788,545 USD.

2020–2021 · Two Consecutive Grants ($788,545)

The winning design features 95 unique mesh pads, each optimized for different impact types. This technology now powers LIGHT helmets present in all 32 NFL locker rooms and ~100 NCAA programs.

Purmundus Challenge | Simulation Driven Design Award

Gabriel Boutin won the inaugural Simulation Driven Design Award at Formnext 2019 for the KUPOL R1-0 motorcycle helmet, competing against 38 finalists from 18 countries.

Formnext 2019 · Frankfurt, Germany

The KUPOL R1-0 achieved sub-15mm protective padding thickness using TPU, a breakthrough impossible with traditional manufacturing. Five complete units print in just 12 hours on HP Jet Fusion.
